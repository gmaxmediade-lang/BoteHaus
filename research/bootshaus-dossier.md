## 14. 3D scan recommendations
Best (opaque, solid, signature, praised):
1. Rolands hausgemachte Maultaschen (14.5): most praised, opaque, distinct shape; scan the geschmaelzte version (onion topping), sauce on the side.
2. Schweineschnitzel Wiener Art (17.5): iconic, matte breading scans well; lemon/cranberry optional.
3. Elsaesser Flammkuchen (14.0): flat, matte, easy from above/oblique.
4. Omas Kaesespaetzle (13.5): praised, matte; small irregular parts, medium difficulty.
5. Schwaebischer Rostbraten / Schoenbuch Schwabenstreich (29.5 / 32.5): signature, opaque; request sauce on the side (gloss).
6. Stueck Kuchen / Torte from Konditorei Frech (4.0-5.0): matte cream, solid, covers the cafe angle.
7. Lauwarmer Schokoladenkuchen (8.5): appealing; scan cake alone, ice cream melts (use a fast pass or a stand-in scoop).
8. Backkartoffel Wellness (12.5): solid; use the Wellness version, not the avocado/salmon ones.
Extras: Hausgemachte Fleischkaechle (13.0, matte, easy).
Avoid: Rinderkraftbruehe (liquid); Eiskaffee/Eisschokolade and melting ice cream; all glass drinks (beer, Hugo, Aperol, wine: transparent and reflective, model in Blender instead); salads (thin leaves, wet sheen); Lachstatar and avocado dishes (glossy, oxidise); Schwaebische Saelze (translucent aspic); anything swimming in sauce (Cognac-Rahmsosse); Wurstsalat unless lightly dressed.
