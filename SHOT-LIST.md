# Bootshaus shoot plan (v1, 2026-10-22)

Sources: research/scroll-pushin-film.md, research/food-3d-capture-pipeline.md, research/bootshaus-dossier.md. PLAUSIBLE values are set by Day 0, not promised.

## Day 0: pilot (2 h, before anything else)
1. Scan 1 dish (Kaesespaetzle or Maultaschen) on a turntable: 3 rings x 56 stills. Also orbit the same plate with the camera for a splat.
2. Shoot a 5 s gimbal push through the glass door, 16:9 and vertical.
3. Claude turns the pilot into: a GLB (and a splat), a 5 s scrub test page, measured MB and fps on an iPhone. Max approves the look before the full shoot.

## Day 1: push-in film (blue hour, about 20-30 min of usable light)
| # | Segment | Rig | Join to next |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Oberer See / dock, slow push toward the beer garden | Gimbal (drone only if allowed, UNKNOWN) | Push into a chestnut trunk (dark) |
| S2 | Beer garden under the chestnuts, toward the lit glass front | Gimbal | Match cut on the door frame |
| S3 | Through the door, interior, to a set table | Gimbal | Blur whip |
| S4 | Hands plating Maultaschen / Schoenbuch beer pour, 100 fps | Slider | Push into the plate |
| S5 | Plate close-up, then fade to dark (word TBD) | Slider | End |
- Settings: 25p, 1/50, 4K, S-Log3 at the correct base ISO. Constant walking pace. 5+ takes per segment, start and end marks taped.
- Every segment also shot as a vertical pass for mobile. Clean glass; polarizer or shoot at an angle (reflections).
- Room: interior lights on. Guests only with written consent (GDPR).

## Day 2: dish scans (controlled room, before service)
- Scan: Maultaschen, Wiener Schnitzel, Flammkuchen, Kaesespaetzle, Rostbraten, cake slice, warm chocolate cake (without ice cream).
- Skip: broth, ice cream, all glass drinks, salads, tartare, aspic, saucy dishes.
- Per dish: hero plate + backup, white base, soft light; cross-polarized for sauces. Manual exposure, grey card, f/11-f/16 on a tripod, intervalometer 2 s. Under 5 min per dish.
- One plain, real table surface: shoot it flat (top-down, even light) for the 3D table texture.
